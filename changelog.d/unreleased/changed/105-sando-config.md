- **The locales are described in `Sando.toml`.** The hand-written table of 36 locales, their endonyms, places and directions in `config.rs` is replaced by `[i18n]` in `Sando.toml`, which sando's build script checks against the catalogue directories and resolves from CLDR, with overrides for Tibetan and Divehi. A locale listed without a catalogue, or a catalogue for an unlisted locale, now fails the build. `dist/` is byte-identical to the build before the change.
