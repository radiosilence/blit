- **just replaces Task**, matching the other radiosilence repositories. Recipes keep their names with `-` for `:` (`just i18n-sync`, `just docker-image`), `format` is now `fmt`, and `just changelog --stdout` takes its arguments without `--`. `just dev` reruns the build through watchexec instead of Task's checksums, so a catalogue whose bytes would not change is no longer rewritten; replacing it was an event that started the next extraction.
