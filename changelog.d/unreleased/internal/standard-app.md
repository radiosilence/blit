- **The site is laid out as a standard app.** The generator moved from `generator/` and `src/` into `crates/blit`, with `templates/`, `styles/`, `assets/` and `locales/` beside its `src/`, as the kōan standard lays out a web UI. Routes map to page modules that render their templates from a shared `Page`, and static generation is a thin layer that renders every route into `dist/`. Each page is a written-out `#[derive(Template)]` struct in place of the `page!` macro, so askama-analyzer analyses every page. The built site is unchanged.
